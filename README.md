# Xen Health Items

A World of Warcraft (Retail) addon that shows a banner when your health drops below a threshold and a healthstone or health potion is ready to use. It works when your health value is secret (instances).

## Features
- Full banner with item icons and keybind, or a simple one-line banner: `USE HEALTHSTONE NOW [key]`
- Shows `NO HEALTH ITEM AVAILABLE [cooldown]` when everything is on cooldown (simple mode)
- Optional click-to-Warning-ping your own health
- Adjustable alert size and warning threshold

## Usage
- `/hpp` or `/xhi` locks and unlocks the banner for moving
- `/hpp test` previews the banner
- `/hpp options` opens the settings
- Minimap button: left-click locks, right-click opens options

Copy the `XenHealthItems` folder into `World of Warcraft/_retail_/Interface/AddOns/`.
